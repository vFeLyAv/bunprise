# Terms of use — Bunprise (pilot)

Version: terms-2026-10-pilot-3 · As of: 25 October 2026

## 1. About the service

Bunprise is a private, free test version for shared occasions, wishlists and secret gift reservations. The provider is Feliks Kolomoiets, email: hello@bunprise.de; further details are in the [legal notice](/legal/impressum?lang=en). Participation is intended for a limited circle of personally invited friends. You can use Bunprise in the browser, as an installed web app or as a Mini App in Telegram; it is the same service with the same account everywhere. The pilot may change or end; see section 10.

## 2. Adults only

Bunprise is intended exclusively for people aged 18 or over. If there are specific indications that a minor is using the service, we investigate and may temporarily restrict access. If underage use is confirmed, we end participation and delete the data under our deletion procedure. Do not create accounts for other people.

## 3. Your account

- One person, one account. Your email address must be reachable; we send your sign-in codes there. Where offered, you can also sign in with Google; Google's terms also apply to this. Your legal name is not required. Choose your display name yourself.
- In Telegram you sign in only with the code from the email. Use in Telegram requires a Telegram account; Telegram's terms also apply to this. Bunprise never asks for your Telegram password or a Telegram code.
- Keep your sign-in codes safe and inform us if you suspect unauthorised access. You are liable for breaches of your duties under the applicable law.
- Do not create additional accounts to follow yourself, evade limits or find out about reservations on your wishes.
- Do not share invitation or follow links with unauthorised people. You can delete your account in settings at any time.

## 4. How the service works

- You create your own occasions and wishlists and invite friends by link. After an explicit decision to join, members of the same occasion can see your display name, attendance status and, if set, your profile picture and banner photo.
- Wishlists without an occasion are visible only to you at first (“Only me”). You decide whether to share them with approved followers (“My followers”). Occasion lists remain with the occasion's members.
- Following is only possible with approval. You can remove followers; they are not notified, although they may notice the loss of access.
- Invited members and, on “My followers” lists, approved followers can reserve wishes. Other eligible people see the status “Reserved”, without the reserver's name. In the app and the automatic data download, you do not see reservations relating to your own wishes.
- On lists without an occasion, you can reserve at most five wishes of the same person at once. If your access to a list ends, or a wish is moved to a list you cannot see, your reservation ends.
- We assess statutory access requests separately. Where information also concerns other people, we consider their rights and freedoms and restrict access only to the extent permitted by law. Your statutory data protection rights remain unaffected.
- A reservation is an indication between friends. It does not create an obligation to buy a gift. We cannot guarantee that duplicate gifts will never be bought.
- Hosts can switch on a “Mitbringen” (bring-along) list. All members of the occasion, including the host, can see who said they will bring what – unlike reservations. Such a commitment is an agreement among friends and creates no obligation. **[REVIEW]**
- You can mark a wish as “Received”; eligible people then see it in the “Received” section, and it cannot be newly reserved. You can move wishes between your own lists.
- When you paste a shop link, our server suggests a title, price, details and a product photo; instead of the product photo, you can choose a photo of your own. This information may be incomplete or wrong; check it before saving and always check prices in the shop. “Find it elsewhere” contains only search links to other providers, without prices.
- Shop links lead to third-party providers. Purchases take place only with those providers; we are not involved and do not process payments. Prices and availability are determined by the shop. There are no advertisements or affiliate links.
- Product ideas under “Ideas” are non-binding suggestions. They are real products from real shops, chosen by us. Prices are approximate, with a date, and may change; you can only buy from the shop itself. We are not paid for this. Paid placements or affiliate links would only be activated after separate setup and with the required labelling (“Anzeige”).

## 6. Invitation and follow links

Anyone holding a valid invitation link can explicitly join the occasion. Merely opening a link does not join a group. Share links only with the people you intend to invite. Hosts can remove members and renew invitation links. Removed members need a new link and must not be in a blocked relationship with the host.

A follow link only permits a request, never automatic following; you still decide on every request. A follow link is valid for at most 30 days and up to 20 new requests. Share it carefully; you can disable it at any time.

## 7. Reporting and blocking

In the pilot, you can report content using “Report” to hello@bunprise.de. Hosts can remove members and renew invitation links.

You can block people. Following relationships and open requests in both directions then end, as do direct occasion memberships and reservations between the two of you. You may both remain members of a third person's occasion but cannot see each other there. The blocked person is not notified. You can remove a block later; ended relationships, memberships and reservations do not come back as a result.

We review reports. Our target response time is one week. If we remove content or restrict an account, we explain the reason to the affected person where legally permitted. Either side can reply by email and we will review the matter again. We may notify the competent authorities where there are indications of a criminal offence.
