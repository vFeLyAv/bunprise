## 2. The essentials

- Bunprise is a private pilot for a limited circle of personally invited friends aged 18 or over.
- We process data for your account, occasions, wishlists, invitations, reservations and the security of the service.
- There are no advertisements, advertising trackers, behavioural analytics, profiling, automated decisions or sales of your data. The pilot is evaluated through voluntary feedback.
- You can delete your account yourself and download your main application data. We also handle statutory data protection requests by email.
- Google sign-in, Turnstile and automatic link previews are not enabled in the pilot described here. This notice must be updated before they are enabled.

## 3. What data we process and why

### 3.1 Account and sign-in

We process your email address, your chosen display name, language, registration time, profile setup status, short-lived sign-in codes and session data. We do not collect a date of birth or gender.

The purpose is to provide your account and sign-in. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR, including steps before a contract when you request a code yourself. The documented assessment of interests and limits for mistaken or abusive code requests must be set before launch: <OTP PROTECTION AND INTERESTS ASSESSMENT>.

For a new registration, we delete the provisional account record no later than 25 hours after its creation if no successful sign-in has taken place. Delivery logs, security logs and backups have the separate retention periods stated below. An existing account is not deleted merely because a sign-in code requested later is left unused.

### 3.2 Occasions, wishlists and wishes

The data includes titles, dates, times, time zones, locations as text, descriptions, wishes with a title, price, currency, link, note and priority, and creation and update times. It also includes membership, role, joining time and attendance status (RSVP).

We use this information for shared occasions and lists. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Members of the same occasion see your display name and attendance status. An occasion's content is intended for its active members. After leaving or being removed, former members do not receive new content from that occasion.

Do not create accounts or wishlists for other people. Do not enter personal information about people who are not participating themselves. For locations, use the name of a public meeting place where possible instead of another person's private address. Please do not enter health information or other particularly sensitive information about yourself or anyone else.

### 3.3 Invitations

We process a random invitation link, its hash, the associated occasion, the inviter, expiry, revocation, number of uses and the resulting membership. You share the link yourself outside the app; creating a link does not require you to provide the recipient's contact details.

The purpose is access to the occasion. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Anyone holding a valid link can explicitly join. Opening the link alone does not create membership. Before joining, we explain that your display name and attendance status will become visible to the members.

### 3.4 Reservations

We process the associations with the wish, list, recipient and reserver, the time, status, any ending reason and a stored wish title. The purpose is secret gift-giving and avoiding duplicate gifts; the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Other active members see “Reserved”, without the reserver's name. The reserver sees their own reservations. In the app and the automatic data download, you do not see reservations relating to your own wishes. We assess statutory access requests separately. Where information also concerns other people, we consider their rights and freedoms and restrict access only to the extent permitted by law. Your statutory data protection rights remain unaffected.

If a wish is deleted or a membership ends, the title stored when the reservation was made may remain in the personal reservation history for up to 90 days after it ends. Former members do not receive updated titles or information about an occasion they can no longer access. The records are then removed by the daily cleanup; see section 6.

### 3.5 Terms, age declaration and privacy information

We store the version of the accepted terms of use, the declaration “at least 18 years old”, the version of the privacy information shown, the server-recorded time and the language. The purpose is evidence of the terms and the information provided. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR; our legitimate interest is maintaining this evidence.

The privacy notice provides information; it is not consent to processing. Agreement to it is not required. Even without accepting new terms of use, downloading your data, deleting your account and exercising your data protection rights remain available.

### 3.6 Reports and safety

In the pilot, you can report content using “Report” to <REPORT-E-MAIL>. We process the sender, the content concerned, the reason and handling status. Hosts can remove members and renew invitation links. A personal blocking feature is not yet available in the pilot.

The purposes are protecting participants and enforcing the terms of use. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; our legitimate interests are a safe service and handling abuse.

### 3.7 Technical access data

When you access the app or its interfaces, hosting, database and email providers may process your IP address, browser information, time and requested address. The purposes are delivery, protection against attacks and troubleshooting. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR; the legitimate interests are security and correct operation. We do not use this data for advertising or behavioural analytics. Actual provider retention periods must be completed in section 6 before launch.

### 3.8 Contact by email

If you write to us, we process the sender and message to respond. Contractual support is based on Article 6(1)(b) GDPR; other enquiries are based on paragraph (f), with the legitimate interest of handling your request. Article 6(1)(c) GDPR applies to compliance with legal obligations that actually apply.

## 6. Retention and deletion

- Account, your own occasions, lists and wishes: until the respective item or the account is deleted from the active application database. Memberships and invitations remain until the occasion or account is deleted; expiry of a link alone does not delete its stored record.
- New provisional account records without a successful sign-in: no later than 25 hours after creation, through hourly cleanup. The production configuration and monitoring must be confirmed before launch.
- Sign-in codes: 10 minutes; session information until sign-out, expiry or revocation. The configuration must be confirmed before launch.
- Invitation links: valid until revocation or expiry, normally for at least 14 days and until seven days after the occasion starts. Renewing a link revokes the previous active invitation.
- Active reservations: until they end or the account is deleted. Ended reservations, including the stored title, are removed by daily cleanup after 90 days, at the next daily run. When an associated account is deleted, its reservations are removed.
- Records of terms, age and privacy information: until account deletion.
- Handled reports stored in the application: removed by daily cleanup after 90 days, at the next daily run; your own reports are deleted with your account.
- Backups: <CONFIRM PROVIDER, LOCATION, ENCRYPTION AND MAXIMUM RETENTION>. Deleted data may remain in backups until then; deletions must be reapplied following restoration.
- Technical authentication, API, hosting and delivery logs and email previews: <SUPABASE, CLOUDFLARE AND BREVO PERIODS AND DELETION PROCEDURES>. Account deletion does not automatically delete these.
- Contact emails: <SET RETENTION AFTER HANDLING AND DELETION PROCEDURE>.

## 7. Local storage

We store session information in your browser for sign-in. We also use session storage for ongoing invitation and sign-in flows. Settings you choose, such as language and reduced animations, may remain stored locally. On sign-out, we remove sign-in data and personal caches; language and motion settings remain.

The personal offline list is off by default. Only if you enable “Quick start: save list offline” in settings is your occasion list stored in the browser for up to 24 hours. Reservation status and your profile are not persistently stored as part of this feature. Disabling it or signing out removes the saved list snapshot. Public application code may remain cached for offline startup. Cookies and storage operations in the actual production configuration must be checked before launch.
