### 3.2 Occasions, wishlists and wishes

The data includes titles, dates, times (start and, optionally, end), time zones, locations as text and, where applicable, the coordinates of a suggested place (section 3.12), descriptions, wishes with a title, price, currency, link, note and priority (“How much do you want it?”), and creation and update times. If you mark a wish as “Received”, people with access to the list see it in the “Received” section; new reservations are then no longer possible. You can move a wish to another list of your own; its details, photo and “Received” mark move with it. For the effect on reservations, see section 3.4.

One wish in several lists: you can also add a wish from your lists to the wishlist of one of your occasions. It stays the same wish (same details, same photo, one shared reservation). Anyone allowed to see at least one of these lists can see it. Guests of the occasion see the note that the wish is also in another list, and that list's name only if they may see that list themselves; followers see no such note. We use this information for shared occasions and lists. The legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. Members of the same occasion see your display name, your attendance status, your commitments in the “Mitbringen” list (section 3.15) and, if set, your profile picture and banner photo (section 3.10). ### 3.4 Reservations

We process the associations with the wish, list, recipient and reserver, the time, status, any ending reason and a stored wish title. The purpose is secret gift-giving and avoiding duplicate gifts; the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR.

Active members of an occasion and, on “My followers” lists, approved followers can reserve. Other eligible people see “Reserved”, without the reserver's name. The reserver sees their own reservations. In the app and the automatic data download, you do not see reservations relating to your own wishes. We assess statutory access requests separately. Where information also concerns other people, we consider their rights and freedoms and restrict access only to the extent permitted by law. Your statutory data protection rights remain unaffected.

On lists without an occasion, you can reserve at most five wishes of the same person at once; reservations of wishes already marked as received do not count, nor do reservations of wishes you also see in the list of an occasion you are a guest of. We check the limit when you make a new reservation; after later changes, for example when a wish is moved to another list, existing reservations may exceed it. If you leave an occasion yourself, at most the five oldest of your counted reservations for the same person remain; we end newer ones, even if you can still see the wishes. If your access to a reserved wish ends, for example because the list is set to “Only me”, you unfollow or are removed, a block exists, the wish is deleted or moved to a list you cannot see, or the list is deleted, the app immediately shows the reservation to you only as “No longer available”, without a reason and without details of the list or occasion; if the wish is still in another list you may see, your reservation continues. An automatic cleanup in the database that runs every minute then ends such reservations for good, usually within a few minutes. If your access returns before it has done so, the reservation continues; later access does not restore a reservation that has ended. If you leave an occasion or unfollow yourself, we immediately end the reservations you thereby lose access to. Nobody can newly reserve a wish marked as received; an existing reservation stays visible to the reserver.

After a reservation ends, the title stored when it was made may remain in the personal reservation history for up to 90 days; as the reason, the app only shows that you left or unfollowed, otherwise “No longer available”. Updated titles or information about an occasion or list that can no longer be accessed are not loaded. The records are then removed by the daily cleanup; see section 6. If you delete your account, or the person whose wish is reserved deletes theirs, the reservations concerned disappear from the app immediately; the automatic cleanup deletes them from the database, usually within a few minutes. Until then, a new data download of the reserving person may still contain the status, times and a general reason, but no wish title and no details of the receiving person, the list or the occasion. Pages already shown and data downloads made earlier are not recalled by this. **[REVIEW: “every minute” and “within a few minutes” only hold while the scheduled task in the database (pg_cron) runs; whether this counts as erasure without undue delay.]**

### 3.11 Link previews, product photos and shop icons

Link preview: when you paste a shop link into a wish, the app sends the URL to our server at Cloudflare. The server removes known tracking parameters, resolves the host name via Cloudflare's DNS service for a security check and fetches the public page using its own connection; for Amazon links it requests a shortened product address. It does not forward your sign-in data, browser cookies, browser referrer or IP address as request headers to the shop. It reads the title, price in euros, shop name, description, brand, colour, product number (GTIN/EAN) and the address of a product image as text; the shop page's HTML is not taken into the app. You can change or delete the suggestions before saving. The URL you enter may still contain personal information; please use only public product links without personal access data. If you tap “Paste”, the app reads your device's clipboard once, as far as your browser allows, and takes only a link found in it; the rest of the text stays on your device and is not stored.

Product photo: if the page names a product image, our server fetches this one image from the shop or its image server, with fixed request headers and without your data, checks file type and size and passes only the image data to the app. The app resizes it on your device as described in section 3.10; when the wish is saved, we store it in the project database. ### 3.12 Place search, map preview and map links

When you search in the place field of an occasion, our server sends the search text and language to the public Photon service (photon.komoot.io), which uses OpenStreetMap data. In the Russian and the Ukrainian interface, German is sent as the language. As the search focus we use fixed coordinates in Germany, not your location; no location permission is requested. Our server does not forward your sign-in data, cookies or IP address to Photon. A name, category, a text description and the place's coordinates (rounded to about one metre) are returned to the app. You can also enter the place by hand. Please search for public places and do not enter other people's private addresses. Recently used places are taken from your own occasions; they are not stored separately for this.

When you choose a suggested place, we store its coordinates with the place on the occasion. From them, the app shows a small map preview with a marker in the form and on the occasion's page, for you and your guests. Only people who can see the place can see the coordinates (the host and the active members of the occasion); they do not appear in the “Start” overview. If you type a different place yourself or take a different, recently used place, we store no coordinates and delete the previous ones; if the place stays unchanged, its coordinates stay too. If you remove the place, we delete its coordinates as well. If you choose your own home address this way, your guests see its exact location.

Our server loads the map images from the OpenStreetMap Foundation (tile.openstreetmap.org) and passes them on to the app; your device does not connect to OpenStreetMap for this. The OpenStreetMap Foundation only receives the numbers of the map images needed – that is, the approximate area of the place –, our server's connection data and an identifier of our app with our contact address, not your IP address, sign-in data, cookies or details of your occasion. We keep map images without any link to you in our server's cache at Cloudflare (up to 60 days); the app keeps them in memory while they are shown and discards them 30 minutes later; your browser may keep them in its cache for up to 7 days. Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL licence.

The Apple Maps and Google Maps buttons open the entered place with the respective provider; only then do you leave the app, and its privacy notice applies. The app does not embed interactive maps.

### 3.15 Bring-along list (“Mitbringen”)

### 3.16 Invitations inside the app

## 9. Must you provide data?

There is no legal obligation to create a Bunprise account. To use the service, we need a reachable email address, a display name and confirmation that you are at least 18 years old. Without this information, we cannot enable participation. Your legal name is not required. You provide other content voluntarily, such as photos, wish details or following relationships; required fields are marked in the app.

## 10. Security

The service uses encrypted connections and access rules in the database. Photos can only be retrieved through database functions that check access, and the functions of our server require sign-in, except fetching the catalogue photos and the calendar subscription, whose secret address itself is the key (section 3.7). We protect access to the administrator accounts at our service providers with two-factor authentication where the provider offers it. To be able to restore data after a technical incident, we create an encrypted backup every week (section 6).

## 11. Minors

Bunprise is intended exclusively for people aged 18 or over. We do not collect a date of birth. If there are specific indications that a minor is using the service, we investigate and may temporarily restrict access. If underage use is confirmed, we end participation and delete the data under our deletion procedure.
